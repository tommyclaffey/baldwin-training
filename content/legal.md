# Privacy Policy

**Effective [Date].** Mise Academy is a training app for restaurant teams, run by [Company]. A restaurant group (the "company") signs up and invites its staff. This explains what we keep and why.

## What we keep
- **Your account:** name, role, store, jobs, start date, when you last used the app. Owners and managers also give an email address. Staff sign in with a join code and a PIN.
- **Passwords and PINs** are stored only as one-way hashes. We can't read them.
- **Your training:** quiz answers and scores, lessons read, sign-offs, typed-name acknowledgments, certificates, notes your manager writes.
- **Your work in the app:** Team Chat messages, reactions and photos; prep counts, batches and waste; anything your company adds or edits (menus, uploads, floor sections).
- **An activity log** of who changed what, and when.
- **Short-term security records:** addresses and emails used to sign in, kept about a day to stop password guessing. Error reports, tied to your account, so we can fix problems.

## What we don't do
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We don't sell or share your data.
- Your company's data is separate from every other company's.

## Who else touches it
- **Railway** hosts the app and its database (United States).
- **Resend** sends password-reset and invite emails, if your company uses them.
- **Anthropic (Claude)** reads a menu file when a manager uses Import a Menu. Only the menu file is sent.
- **Google Fonts and YouTube** load fonts and training videos; they see your device's address when they do.

## How long we keep it
- As long as your company uses Mise Academy.
- Backups: daily copies for 14 days and an encrypted nightly copy for 30 days. Deleted data leaves the backups when they age out.

## Your choices
- **Owners** can download all of the company's data (Admin → You → Company Data) or ask for it to be deleted. Deletion happens after a 7-day wait and a second check on our side.
- **Staff:** ask your manager, or email [Support email]. Your employer decides what training records it keeps, the same as paper training files.

1. **The service.** We host the app, keep it running and back it up. We can change features; we'll tell owners before we remove anything they use.
2. **Your content stays yours.** Menus, recipes, photos, videos and training you add belong to your company. You give us permission to store and show them to your team, nothing more.
3. **Accuracy is yours to confirm.** Training content, allergen information and anything the menu reader drafts must be checked by your managers. The app marks AI drafts and allergens "to confirm" for this reason. Mise Academy isn't a substitute for your kitchen's allergen procedures or food-safety law.
4. **Certificates** are training certificates from your restaurant. They aren't certifications from any outside body (USBG, Court of Master Sommeliers, Cicerone or others).
5. **Accounts.** Owners control who has access and remove people who leave. Don't share sign-ins.
6. **Fair use.** Don't try to reach another company's data, overload the service or copy the app.

8. **Ending.** Either side can end with 30 days' notice. Download your data before the end; we delete it after.
9. **Limits.** The service is provided as is. Our total liability is limited to what you paid in the last 12 months.
10. **Law.** [State] law applies.
